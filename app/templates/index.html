How it works

Clean, safe, and thorough

🗑️

Trash, not gone

Duplicates are moved to your cloud trash bin — restore them any time if you change your mind.

🔍

Exact & near-duplicates

Finds pixel-identical copies and visually similar versions — recompressed, resized, or re-uploaded.

⚡

Metadata-first

Compares file hashes and perceptual fingerprints without downloading your entire photo library.

☁️

Multi-cloud

Scan Google Drive, Google Photos, and OneDrive in a single pass to catch cross-service duplicates.
